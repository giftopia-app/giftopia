# Giftopia Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Giftopia is a free desktop app for TikTok LIVE creators. It shows alerts, sounds, text-to-speech, overlays and other tools during your streams. This policy explains what information Giftopia uses and where it goes. Short version: **Giftopia keeps your data on your own computer. We don't run accounts, we don't sell data, and we don't track you.**

## 1. What stays on your computer

Everything Giftopia saves is stored **only on your PC**, in Giftopia's data folder (`%APPDATA%\Giftopia` or `%APPDATA%\Rosefire`). This includes:

- Your settings, actions, overlays, sound alerts, timers and goals
- The TikTok username you connect to
- Your Euler Stream API key, if you add one
- Viewer stats Giftopia builds while you stream (for example points, top gifters and likes), including viewers' public TikTok usernames, nicknames and profile pictures
- Your Spotify login token, if you connect Spotify
- Sound files and gift pictures it downloads

We (the Giftopia developer) can't see any of this. It isn't uploaded anywhere by Giftopia. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling Giftopia and deleting the data folder above.

## 2. Services Giftopia talks to

To work, Giftopia connects to some outside services. When it does, that service receives the information below and handles it under its **own** privacy policy.

| Service | When | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | When you connect to a LIVE | The TikTok username you connect to, plus normal web request info (like your IP address). Giftopia then receives the LIVE's public events: chat, gifts, likes, follows and so on. |
| **Euler Stream** (eulerstream.com) | When you connect to a LIVE | The LIVE you're connecting to, and your Euler API key if you added one. It's used to make the connection signature TikTok needs. |
| **GitHub** | When Giftopia checks for updates (on start and every few hours) | Normal web request info (like your IP address), so it can download new versions. |
| **Spotify** | Only if you connect Spotify | Your Spotify login, and requests for song info and song requests. |
| **MyInstants** | When you search for or play sounds from its library | The sound you search for or play. |
| **Google Fonts** | When the app's screens load | Normal web request info, to load the fonts. |
| **Services you set up yourself** | Only if you turn them on | Whatever you configure, for example IFTTT or webhooks, Streamer.bot, OBS or a Minecraft server. |

Text-to-speech uses the voices built into Windows, on your computer.

## 3. What we don't do

- No Giftopia accounts and no sign-up with us
- No analytics, tracking or ads
- We don't sell, rent or share your information
- We don't collect your viewers' information. Viewer stats stay on your PC.

## 4. Your viewers

When you stream with Giftopia, it receives the public events of your LIVE, the same things anyone watching can see: usernames, nicknames, profile pictures, chat messages and gifts. These stay on your PC and are used only to run your alerts, overlays and stats. As the streamer, you decide what is shown on screen.

## 5. Children

Giftopia is meant for people old enough to use TikTok LIVE under TikTok's own rules. It isn't made for children.

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Giftopia ever starts sending information somewhere new, for example a Giftopia connection server, this page will say so before that update is released.
